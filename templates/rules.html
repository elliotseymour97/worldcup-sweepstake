{# ── The Draw ── #}

The Draw

All 48 World Cup countries are split into 4 tiers based on FIFA ranking and tournament seeding. Each of the 6 players is randomly assigned 2 countries per tier — 8 countries each.

Tier Multipliers

Lower-ranked teams earn more points per win to balance the draw — an underdog win is worth nearly twice a favourite's win.

{# ── Points per match ── #}

Points per Match

Points are awarded for each finished match, then multiplied by the country's tier multiplier.

3 Win × tier multiplier
1 Draw (group stage only) × tier multiplier
0 Loss / knockout draw

Example

A ×1.75 Tier 4 team wins in the group stage: 3 × 1.75 = 5.25 pts
{# ── Stage bonuses ── #}

Stage Bonuses

A flat bonus is added on top of each win (or group-stage draw) to reward teams that go further in the tournament.

{# ── Tiebreakers ── #}

Tiebreakers

If two players are level on points, the league table uses:

  1. 1.Total points
  2. 2.Goal difference (goals scored − goals conceded across all countries)
  3. 3.Furthest round reached by any of their countries
